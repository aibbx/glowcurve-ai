Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 21, 2026

1. Introduction

GlowCurve AI ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the GlowCurve AI mobile application (the "App").

2. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address or Apple ID identifier, and a display name you choose.

Photos: When you use the beauty scoring feature, your selfie is processed to generate a score. Photos are processed on-device and via our secure AI service. We do not store your original photos on our servers unless you explicitly choose to share them on the social feed.

Usage Data: We collect beauty scores, timestamps, and tags (e.g., "bare face," "post-workout") that you assign to each capture to power your personal GlowCurve AI chart.

Purchase Information: If you make in-app purchases, payment processing is handled entirely by Apple. We receive a purchase confirmation but never see your payment details.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide beauty scoring, tracking, and celebrity matching features
  • To display your personal score history and trends
  • To enable social sharing features you opt into
  • To improve our AI models and app experience (using anonymized, aggregated data only)

4. Data Storage and Security

Your account data is stored securely on Supabase infrastructure with encryption at rest and in transit. We use row-level security policies to ensure you can only access your own data.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — authentication and database hosting
  • Apple Sign In — optional authentication provider
  • Apple StoreKit — in-app purchase processing

These services have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your personal data with third parties except as necessary to operate the services described above, or as required by law.

7. Social Features

If you post to the GlowCurve AI social feed, your display name, score, and shared image become visible to other users. You can delete your posts at any time.

8. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us at the email below.

9. Children's Privacy

GlowCurve AI is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn we have collected such information, we will delete it promptly.

10. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Export your data in a portable format

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes through the App or via email.
