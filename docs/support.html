Frequently Asked Questions

How does the beauty scoring work?

GlowCurve AI uses advanced AI to analyze your selfie across four dimensions: Symmetry, Skin Clarity, Proportions, and Vibe. Your overall GlowScore is a composite of these factors.

Are my photos stored on your servers?

Photos are processed to generate your score but are not permanently stored on our servers unless you choose to share them on the social feed.

How do credits work?

You start with 50 free credits. Earn more through daily check-ins, streaks, sharing, and inviting friends. You can also purchase credit packs or subscribe to VIP for 3,000 credits per month.

How do I cancel my VIP subscription?

You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime through your device's Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

How do I delete my account?

To delete your account and all associated data, please contact us at support@glowcurve.app.
